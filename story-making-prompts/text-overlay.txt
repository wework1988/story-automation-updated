Act as a professional YouTube documentary editor.

I will provide a scene-by-scene documentary script. Your task is to identify only the most important scenes where a short text overlay will help viewers understand the date, time, location, famous person, organisation, operation, major event, statistic, weapon, or historical context.

## Select overlays for

Choose a scene only when it contains:

1. Important date, year, time, or historical period
2. City, country, battlefield, airport, route, or major location
3. Famous person, ruler, scientist, officer, leader, agent, or victim
4. Major attack, assassination, rescue, war, discovery, or turning point
5. Intelligence operation, military mission, investigation, or secret project
6. Intelligence agency, army, government body, or militant organisation
7. Important number, distance, casualty count, duration, or milestone
8. Important quote, order, demand, warning, or decision
9. Important weapon, aircraft, technology, or technical system

## Overlay frequency

### Scenes 1–50

The opening section may contain more overlays because viewers are still learning the setting, people, dates, and organisations.

* Select approximately 8–12 overlays.
* Do not select every scene.
* Avoid unnecessary overlays in consecutive scenes.
* Keep at least 2–3 scenes between normal overlays whenever possible.

### After Scene 50

* Select approximately one overlay in every 15-scene block.
* Select a maximum of two only when the block contains two very important events.
* Skip a block if there is nothing important.
* Do not add weak overlays just to complete the frequency.
* Do not repeat the same name, location, date, or operation unnecessarily.

## Text overlay rules

* Keep every overlay short and easy to read.
* Prefer 2–7 words.
* Maximum 10 words.
* Do not copy the full narration.
* Use exact dates, names, locations, and numbers where important.
* Combine date, time, and location when they appear in the same scene.
* Add a short designation after a famous name when necessary.
* Do not reveal information before the narration introduces it.
* Avoid vague or generic overlays such as:

  * A Dangerous Mission
  * The Story Begins
  * Something Shocking Happened
  * What Happened Next?

## Required output format

Return only a simple list.

Use exactly this format:

Scene Number: Text Overlay

Example:

Scene 1: 27 नवंबर 2020 | अबसर्द, ईरान | 3:24 PM
Scene 5: Mohsen Fakhrizadeh | Nuclear Scientist
Scene 19: Nissan Truck Destroyed
Scene 30: Operation Diamond

Do not include:

* Overlay type
* Screen location or placement
* Reason or explanation
* Scene-block headings
* Additional editing advice
* Script summary

Before giving the final output, check that:

* The first 50 scenes contain only the strongest 8–12 overlays.
* After Scene 50, there is approximately one overlay per 15 scenes.
* Repeated or unnecessary overlays are removed.
* Scene numbers exactly match the supplied script.
* All selected scenes are arranged in chronological order.

Now analyse the complete script and provide only the final overlay list.
